Superherooo Insurance Policy

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

At Superherooo, we prioritize your peace of mind. Every service booked through our platform is backed by our comprehensive insurance policy.

1. The Guarantee

If a Superherooo Independent Professional damages your property during a booked service, we provide coverage up to ₹50,000 for eligible claims.

2. Eligibility

To be eligible for the insurance claim:

  • The service must have been booked and paid for entirely through the Superherooo Platform.
  • The damage must have occurred as a direct result of the Professional's negligence during the service.
  • The claim must be reported within 24 hours of the service completion.

3. Exclusions

The policy does not cover:

  • Pre-existing damages or normal wear and tear.
  • Cash, jewelry, fine art, or rare collectibles.
  • Services arranged outside of the Superherooo platform.

4. Claim Process

To file a claim, please take clear photos of the damage immediately and contact our support team. We will send an inspector within 48 hours to assess the damage and process the reimbursement.

5. Contact Us

For claims or questions regarding this policy, please email insurance@superherooo.com.

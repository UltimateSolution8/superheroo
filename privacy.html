Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to SUPERHEROOO’s privacy policy (“Privacy Policy” or “Policy”).

SUPERHEROOO (“SUPERHEROOO”, “Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) operates a technology platform that connects customers seeking temporary, daily-wage, or unskilled and semi-skilled labour services — such as loaders, cleaners, construction helpers, event staff, and housekeeping personnel — with verified labour workers and authorised mediators, across venues such as malls, gated communities, offices, construction sites, and events (collectively, the “Services”). This Policy outlines our practices in relation to the collection, storage, usage, processing, and disclosure of personal data that you consent to share with us when you access, use, or otherwise interact with our website or our mobile application (collectively, the “Platform”).

At SUPERHEROOO, we are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. In order to provide you with access to the Services, we have to collect and otherwise process certain data about you. This Policy explains how we process and use personal data about you, and applies equally to Customers, Labour Workers, Mediators, and visitors to the Platform.

By registering on or using the SUPERHEROOO Platform, you confirm that you have read and agree to be bound by this Policy, and consent to the collection and processing activities described herein.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

(a) This Policy applies to all individuals who access or use the SUPERHEROOO Platform, including:

  • Customers who book labour services through the Platform.
  • Labour Workers who register to offer their services through the Platform.
  • Mediators who are authorised to coordinate job assignments between Customers and Workers. and
  • Visitors who browse or otherwise interact with the Platform without creating an account.

(b) This Policy applies whenever you use our mobile application, our website, our customer support channels, or any other service offered through SUPERHEROOO, regardless of the device you use to access it.

(c) Because SUPERHEROOO serves both Customers and Workers, certain categories of information described below (for example, KYC documents and worker skill data) are collected only from Labour Workers and Mediators, and not from Customers. Where a category applies to a specific type of user, this is noted accordingly.

2. Information We Collect

(a) Personal Information

When you register on the Platform, whether as a Customer or as a Labour Worker, we collect basic identity and contact information that allows us to create your account and communicate with you. This includes:

  • Full Name.
  • Mobile Number, used for OTP-based verification and communication.
  • Email Address (optional), used for account-related and service notifications.
  • Date of Birth, used to confirm that you meet our minimum age requirement of 18 years.
  • Gender.
  • Residential Address. and
  • Profile Photograph, used to help Customers and Workers identify one another at the time of service delivery.

(b) Worker Information

In addition to the Personal Information above, Labour Workers who register to offer services through the Platform are required to share additional occupational information so that we can match them with suitable job opportunities. This includes:

  • Worker ID, a unique identifier generated on registration.
  • Skills and job categories the Worker is trained or experienced in (e.g. loading, cleaning, painting, plumbing assistance).
  • Experience, including years worked and prior job types.
  • Preferred Work Categories, indicating the type of assignments the Worker wishes to be matched with.
  • Languages Spoken, to help with communication at the job site.
  • Daily Wage Expectations, used for job matching and fair compensation display to Customers.
  • Availability Status, indicating when the Worker is open to accepting jobs. and
  • Work Location Preferences, indicating the areas or venues (malls, gated communities, offices, construction sites, events, etc.) the Worker is willing to travel to.

(c) Identity Verification (KYC)

Because SUPERHEROOO connects Customers with Workers who will be physically present at their homes, offices, or event venues, maintaining a trusted and verified Platform is central to how we operate. For this reason, we collect the following identity verification information, primarily from Labour Workers and Mediators:

  • Aadhaar Number.
  • PAN Card details, where available.
  • Driving Licence details, where applicable to the role.
  • Other Government-issued Identity Proof. and
  • A Selfie or live Photograph taken at the time of verification, which is matched against the submitted identity document.

This KYC information is collected strictly for the purposes of verifying identity, preventing fraud and impersonation, ensuring the safety of Customers and job sites, and complying with applicable laws. It is not used for any unrelated commercial purpose.

(d) Location Information

With your permission, we collect location data that is essential to how the Platform functions. This includes:

  • GPS Location, used at the time of booking to identify nearby available Workers.
  • Live Location during an active, ongoing job, used for safety and coordination between the Customer, Worker, and, where applicable, the Mediator. and
  • Proximity data used for matching Workers to job requests in their vicinity.

Location access allows Customers to find nearby Workers quickly, allows Mediators to allocate jobs efficiently, and enables attendance and check-in verification at the job site.

(e) Device Information

We automatically collect certain technical information whenever you use the Platform, including:

  • Device Model and Device ID.
  • Operating System and App Version.
  • IP Address and Mobile Network information. and
  • Browser Information, where the Platform is accessed via our website.

(f) Payment Information

To process payments between Customers, Workers, and, where applicable, Mediators, we collect:

  • UPI ID.
  • Bank Account Details, for wage disbursement to Workers.
  • Payment Transaction History. and
  • Wallet Information, where the Platform offers an in-app wallet feature.

SUPERHEROOO does not store complete debit or credit card numbers. card payments, where accepted, are processed by our third-party payment gateway partners in accordance with applicable payment security standards.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information about you through the following means:

  • When you register an account as a Customer, Worker, or Mediator.
  • When you complete the KYC verification process.
  • When you book a labour service or accept a job request.
  • When you contact our customer support team.
  • When you submit documents for verification or dispute resolution.
  • When you enable and use location services on your device. and
  • When you make or receive a payment through the Platform.

In addition to information you provide directly, certain data — such as device identifiers, IP address, and app usage patterns — is collected automatically through cookies and similar device technologies each time you access the Platform.

4. Why We Collect Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • to create and manage your Customer, Worker, or Mediator account.
  • to verify your identity at the time of registration and periodically thereafter.
  • to complete KYC verification in accordance with applicable law.
  • to match Customers with nearby, available, and suitably skilled Workers.
  • to assign Workers to job requests through authorised Mediators, where applicable.
  • to process and manage bookings from request to completion.
  • to enable attendance verification through check-in and check-out at the job site.
  • to process payments and wage disbursement.
  • to provide and improve customer support.
  • to detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, impersonation, or misuse of the Platform.
  • to resolve disputes between Customers, Workers, and Mediators.
  • to monitor and improve the performance, safety, and reliability of the Platform. and
  • to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

5. OTP Verification

SUPERHEROOO uses One-Time Password (OTP) verification, sent to your registered mobile number, to improve the safety, accountability, and transparency of transactions carried out on the Platform. OTP verification may be used at the following stages:

  • User Registration, to confirm ownership of the mobile number provided.
  • Login Verification, to secure access to your account.
  • Job Acceptance, to confirm that a Worker has genuinely accepted an assigned job.
  • Worker Check-in, to confirm that the correct, verified Worker has arrived at the job site.
  • Job Completion Confirmation, to confirm that the service has been satisfactorily delivered. and
  • Payment Authorization, to confirm that a payment or payout has been authorised by the account holder.

This layered OTP process is designed to ensure that services on the Platform are requested, delivered, and paid for only by verified users, reducing the risk of impersonation or fraudulent job claims.

6. Location Tracking

Location information is collected only where it is necessary for the functioning of the Platform, and is used for the following purposes:

  • Finding nearby, available Labour Workers at the time a Customer places a booking.
  • Job allocation, so that Mediators can assign the nearest suitable Worker to a request.
  • Attendance verification, confirming that a Worker has checked in at the correct job site.
  • Route assistance, helping Workers navigate to the job location. and
  • Fraud prevention, by cross-verifying that check-in locations correspond to the booked job site.

Users may choose to disable location services through their device settings at any time. however, certain core features of the Platform — including worker matching, job allocation, and attendance verification — may become unavailable or function with reduced accuracy as a result.

7. How We Share Information

We share limited, purpose-specific information with the following parties, strictly as necessary to deliver the Services:

  • Customers, who receive the name, photograph, and relevant verification status of the Worker assigned to their booking.
  • Labour Workers, who receive the job location, job category, and Customer contact details necessary to complete an assignment.
  • Authorised Mediators, who coordinate job assignments between Customers and Workers.
  • Payment Service Providers, to process payments, payouts, and wallet transactions.
  • SMS Providers, to deliver OTPs and service-related notifications.
  • Cloud Hosting Providers, who store Platform data securely on our behalf. and
  • Government Authorities, where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or a valid legal process.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or to any unrelated third party, and we require every third party we work with to handle your data securely and only for the specific purpose for which it was shared.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL Encryption for data transmitted between your device and our servers.
  • Secure Cloud Storage with restricted access.
  • Password Encryption for all user accounts.
  • OTP Authentication at key stages of the user journey.
  • Role-Based Access Controls, ensuring that internal staff and Mediators can access only the information necessary for their role.
  • Secure APIs for data exchange between the Platform and its service providers. and
  • Continuous Security Monitoring to detect and respond to potential threats.

While we work to protect your information using industry-appropriate safeguards, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we encourage users to also take reasonable steps to protect their own account credentials.

9. Data Retention

Your information is retained by us for the following periods and purposes:

  • For as long as your account remains active on the Platform.
  • For the period required to comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • For as long as necessary to resolve any ongoing disputes between Customers, Workers, or Mediators.
  • As required for fraud prevention and platform safety purposes. and
  • As required to meet tax, accounting, and financial record-keeping requirements.

Once a particular retention period is no longer required, the corresponding personal information will be securely deleted or anonymised such that it can no longer be linked back to you.

10. Cookies

SUPERHEROOO uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your login session across visits.
  • Improve the overall user experience of the Platform.
  • Analyze application performance and usage patterns. and
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity on the Platform.

You may disable cookies at any time through your browser or device settings. however, doing so may cause certain features of the Platform to function incorrectly or become unavailable.

11. Your Rights

As a user of the SUPERHEROOO Platform, you may exercise the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • View the personal information we hold about you.
  • Update your profile information to keep it accurate and current.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent to receive marketing communications. and
  • Contact us directly with any privacy-related concerns or questions.

Please note that certain categories of information, such as KYC and transaction records, may need to be retained even after such a request, where retention is required by applicable law.

12. Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your SUPERHEROOO account at any time by contacting our support team or by using the account deletion option available within the application.

Please note that deleting your account may result in the permanent loss of:

  • Booking History.
  • Worker History and job records.
  • Ratings and reviews associated with your account.
  • Wallet Information and any associated balance. and
  • Saved Preferences.

Notwithstanding account deletion, certain records may continue to be retained where necessary to comply with legal, regulatory, or accounting requirements.

13. Children’s Privacy

The SUPERHEROOO Platform is intended solely for use by individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and any account found to be associated with a minor may be suspended or terminated upon discovery.

Platform Policies & Code of Conduct

The following policies apply in addition to the Privacy Policy above and govern how bookings, conduct, safety, payments, and disputes are handled on the SUPERHEROOO Platform. These policies apply to Customers, Labour Workers, and Mediators alike, except where a policy is expressly limited to one category of user.

17. Cancellation Policy

(a) Customer Cancellations

Cancellation charges applicable to a Customer depend on how much notice is given before the scheduled service time:

Situation Typical Policy
Within 2–5 minutes of bookingFree cancellation
More than 2–4 hours before serviceFree cancellation
Less than 2 hours before service30% cancellation fee
Worker has already started travelling50% — Customer pays travel/cancellation fee
Worker has reached the location80% — Customer pays full visit charge

(b) Labour Worker Cancellations

  • No penalty is applied for genuine emergencies, such as a medical issue or accident.
  • Repeated cancellations reduce a Worker’s acceptance rating.
  • Temporary suspension may follow a pattern of repeated cancellations.
  • Incentives payable to the Worker may be reduced. and
  • Permanent removal from the Platform may result from excessive or unexplained cancellations.

18. Late Arrival Policy

(a) Worker is Late

  • The Customer receives a notification of the delay.
  • A grace period of 10–15 minutes is applied.
  • The Customer has the option to reschedule the booking.
  • The Customer has the option to cancel the booking without any charges. and
  • Repeated lateness affects the Worker’s rating on the Platform.

(b) Customer is Late

  • The Worker will wait at the location for 10–15 minutes.
  • A visit charge applies if the Customer does not respond within this window. and
  • The booking may be marked as a Customer no-show.

19. No Show Policy

(a) Customer No-Show

  • The Worker marks the Customer as absent on the Platform.
  • A visit fee is charged to the Customer. and
  • No refund is applicable in such cases.

(b) Worker No-Show

  • The Customer receives a full refund.
  • Priority rebooking is offered to the Customer. and
  • An internal penalty is recorded against the Worker’s account.

20. Physical Abuse Policy

Physical abuse is treated as one of the most serious violations of this Policy, and applies equally to Customers, Workers, and Mediators.

(a) This includes:

  • Hitting.
  • Slapping.
  • Pushing.
  • Throwing objects.
  • Physical intimidation. and
  • Threatening violence.

(b) Action taken:

  • Immediate suspension of the account involved.
  • A formal investigation into the incident.
  • A permanent ban if the allegation is confirmed.
  • A police complaint filed where required. and
  • No refund processed until the investigation concludes.

21. Verbal Abuse Policy

(a) Examples include:

  • Swearing.
  • Racist comments.
  • Sexist remarks.
  • Continuous shouting.
  • Personal insults. and
  • Threats.

(b) Action taken:

  • A formal warning issued to the account holder.
  • Temporary suspension for confirmed incidents. and
  • A permanent account ban for repeated violations.

22. Sexual Harassment Policy

SUPERHEROOO maintains a zero-tolerance approach to sexual harassment on the Platform.

(a) Examples include:

  • Inappropriate touching.
  • Sexual comments.
  • Asking for personal meetings outside the scope of the booked service.
  • Unwanted advances. and
  • Taking photographs without consent.

(b) Action taken:

  • Immediate account suspension.
  • A police complaint filed where necessary.
  • A permanent ban from the Platform. and
  • Full cooperation with legal authorities where required.

23. Drinking / Alcohol Policy

Labour Workers must:

  • Never arrive at a job site intoxicated.
  • Never consume alcohol during the course of a service. and
  • Never carry alcohol onto the job site unless it is required for the job itself.

If a violation is reported, the following steps are taken:

  • The service is immediately stopped.
  • The Customer receives a refund.
  • The Worker is suspended pending investigation.
  • An investigation is carried out. and
  • Permanent removal from the Platform follows if the violation is confirmed.

Customers who are heavily intoxicated and create an unsafe environment for the Worker may also have their booking cancelled.

24. Drug Policy

The use, possession, or influence of illegal drugs on the Platform is strictly prohibited. This includes:

  • Possession of illegal drugs.
  • Distribution or sale of illegal drugs. and
  • Working or requesting services while under the influence of drugs.

Any confirmed violation results in immediate removal from the Platform, and police involvement where required.

25. Smoking Policy

(a) Labour Workers:

  • Must not smoke inside a Customer’s premises. and
  • Must not smoke during the course of a service.

(b) Customers:

  • Should avoid smoking in an enclosed workspace while a service is ongoing.

Repeated violations of this policy by either party may lead to account restrictions.

26. Property Damage Policy

(a) If a Worker damages property:

  • The incident must be reported immediately.
  • Photographic evidence is required.
  • An investigation is carried out. and
  • Compensation is determined based on the assessed damage.

(b) If a Customer falsely claims damage:

  • An investigation is carried out using available evidence. and
  • False claims may result in penalties or suspension of the Customer’s account.

27. Theft Policy

(a) This includes:

  • Missing valuables at the job site.
  • Stealing cash. and
  • Taking Customer property without consent.

(b) Action taken:

  • Immediate suspension of the account.
  • A formal investigation.
  • A police complaint filed where required. and
  • A permanent ban if the allegation is confirmed.

28. Safety Policy

A Worker may refuse to provide or continue a service where:

  • The environment is unsafe.
  • There is evidence of violence.
  • Weapons are visible at the location.
  • Illegal activity is taking place.
  • The Customer is behaving aggressively. or
  • Hazardous conditions are present at the job site.

The Customer will not be charged where the safety concern is legitimate and verified by the Platform.

29. Fraud Policy

(a) This includes:

  • Fake bookings.
  • Fake payments.
  • Fake reviews.
  • Creation of multiple fake accounts. and
  • Identity fraud.

(b) Action taken:

  • Account suspension.
  • A permanent ban from the Platform. and
  • Legal action where appropriate.

30. Payment Policy

  • Online payment is the preferred mode of payment on the Platform.
  • Cash payments are accepted only where the Platform expressly supports this option.
  • Labour Workers may not demand any amount beyond the price displayed on the Platform. and
  • Any additional work requested at the job site requires Customer approval, either through the Platform or a documented agreement.

31. Rating & Review Policy

Customers are expected to submit reviews that reflect their actual service experience. The following are not permitted:

  • Fake reviews.
  • Offensive language.
  • Personal attacks. and
  • Using ratings to threaten or blackmail a Worker or Mediator.

Violations of this policy may result in removal of the review and action against the account responsible.

32. Labour Worker Code of Conduct

Labour Workers registered on the Platform must:

  • Wear clean attire or uniform, where applicable.
  • Carry a valid ID at all times while on a job.
  • Be polite and respectful towards Customers.
  • Maintain personal hygiene.
  • Follow all Platform guidelines and safety protocols.
  • Protect the privacy of Customer information. and
  • Not solicit work directly from a Customer, outside the Platform, during a booked service.

33. Customer Code of Conduct

Customers using the Platform must:

  • Treat Workers and Mediators respectfully.
  • Provide accurate address and contact details at the time of booking.
  • Ensure a reasonably safe working environment at the job site.
  • Avoid abusive, discriminatory, or threatening behaviour. and
  • Pay all applicable charges for the service availed.

34. Refund Policy

(a) Refunds are typically provided where:

  • The Worker does not arrive for a confirmed booking.
  • The wrong service is delivered.
  • There is a verified quality issue with the service.
  • A safety incident occurs during the service. or
  • A duplicate payment has been made.

(b) Refunds are generally not provided where:

  • The Customer changes their mind after the service has been completed.
  • There is dissatisfaction without supporting evidence. or
  • The Customer cancels after the work has already begun.

35. Identity Verification

(a) Labour Workers and Mediators:

  • Government-issued ID verification.
  • Address verification.
  • Bank account verification.
  • Background check, where available in the relevant location. and
  • Selfie verification matched against submitted ID.

(b) Customers:

  • OTP verification at registration.
  • Mobile number verification. and
  • Optional email verification.

36. Account Suspension & Termination

An account may be suspended or terminated for reasons including:

  • Repeated cancellations.
  • Fraudulent activity.
  • Physical or verbal abuse.
  • Sexual harassment.
  • Theft.
  • Drug or alcohol policy violations.
  • Submission of fake documents. and
  • Consistently low service quality following repeated warnings.

Suspensions may be temporary or permanent, depending on the nature and severity of the violation.

37. Additional Provisions for the SUPERHEROOO Mediator Model

Because SUPERHEROOO connects Customers with Labour Workers through authorised Mediators, the following additional provisions apply:

  • Mediator Responsibility: Mediators are responsible for verifying Worker identity, confirming attendance, and monitoring conduct at the job site.
  • OTP-Based Start & Completion: Work begins only after a start OTP is confirmed, and is marked complete only after a completion OTP is confirmed.
  • Worker Replacement: Where a Worker is unavailable or found unsuitable for the assignment, the Mediator must arrange a replacement within an agreed time.
  • Alcohol & Substance Abuse: Workers found to be under the influence of alcohol or drugs at the worksite will be removed immediately, and repeated incidents may result in permanent deactivation from the Platform.
  • Site Safety: Customers must provide a reasonably safe working environment and any required protective equipment, where applicable to the nature of the job.
  • Dispute Resolution: Payment disputes, misconduct complaints, and damage claims should be reported within a defined period (typically 24–48 hours) and will be investigated by the Platform before a final decision is issued.
  • Commission & Payments: The Platform clearly defines commission deductions, payout timelines, and the conditions under which payments may be withheld during an active investigation.

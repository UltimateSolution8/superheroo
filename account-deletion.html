Document Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Superherooo account and associated personal data from the app or by contacting our support team. You do not need to install an additional app or create another account.

01

Request deletion in the app

  1. Sign in to the relevant Superherooo app.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Request account deletion.
  4. Review the notice and submit the request.
02

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address registered to your account. Include your registered phone number and whether you use the Citizen, Partner, or Mediator app.

Security reminder

Never include your password, OTP, payment credentials, or identity-document images in your email.

Start your deletion request

Contact our support team using your registered email address.

Start Deletion Request
03

What happens next

  • Support verifies account ownership before processing the request.
  • Active jobs, disputes, safety cases, or outstanding settlements may need to be resolved first.
  • Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days.
04

Data deleted

Subject to verification and legal obligations, deletion covers profile and contact information, saved addresses and preferences, device and notification tokens, profile photos, and other account-linked data that is no longer required.

05

Data that may be retained

Limited transaction, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, safety, dispute, KYC, and legal-compliance records may be retained for the period required by applicable law.

Retained records are restricted from normal product use and deleted or anonymized when the applicable retention period ends.

06

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or how your personal data is handled, please review our Privacy Policy or contact our support team.

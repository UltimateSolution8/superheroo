Request Account Deletion

Applies to: Superherooo Citizen, Partner, and Mediator

You can request deletion of your Superherooo account and associated personal data either inside the app or through the web request channel below.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Sign in to the relevant Superherooo app.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Request account deletion.
  4. Review the notice and select Submit request.

The request is routed directly to Admin support and can be tracked in the app’s Help Center.

Request deletion on the web

Email our support team from the email address registered to your account. Include your registered phone number and whether you use the Citizen, Partner, or Mediator app. Never send a password, OTP, payment credential, or identity-document image.

Email: support@superherooo.com

Start deletion request

What happens next

  • Support verifies account ownership before processing the request.
  • Active jobs, unresolved safety cases, disputes, or outstanding settlement items may need to be completed first.
  • After verification, the account is disabled and data that is not legally required is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.
  • We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will notify you if a legal or operational exception requires more time.

Data deleted

Subject to verification and legal obligations, deletion covers profile and contact data, saved addresses and preferences, device and notification tokens, profile photos, and other account-linked data that is no longer required.

Data that may be retained

Limited KYC, transaction, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, safety, dispute, and legal-compliance records may be retained for the period required by applicable law. Retained records are restricted from normal product use and are deleted or anonymised when the applicable retention period ends.

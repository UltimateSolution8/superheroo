11. Android Application Permissions

This section explains the device permissions requested by the SuperHerooo Android application, why each permission is needed, and how the related data is used. Permissions are requested only for functionality that is actually used within the app, in line with the purpose-limitation principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11.1 General Principles

The SuperHerooo Android application follows Android's runtime permission model. This means:

  • Sensitive permissions are requested only at the point they are needed, not all at once during installation
  • Users may grant or deny each permission individually through the Android system permission dialog
  • Users may review or revoke any previously granted permission at any time through Settings > Apps > SuperHerooo > Permissions on their device
  • Denying a permission may disable or limit the specific feature that depends on it, as described below, without necessarily affecting the rest of the app

11.2 Permissions Requested and Their Purpose

Permission Type Purpose
Location (Precise / Approximate) Runtime To detect the Customer's or Expert's location for service address selection, Expert assignment, live tracking of an assigned Expert en route, and to confirm login/logout at a booking's service location for insurance coverage purposes under Clause 16.4.
Camera Runtime To capture photographs for KYC and background verification, profile pictures, before/after service documentation, and to upload images for support tickets or reviews.
Photos, Media and Files / Storage Runtime To select and upload existing images or documents (such as identity proofs) from the device, and to save invoices, receipts, or booking-related documents.
Phone (Call Handling) Runtime To enable in-app masked calling between Customers and assigned Experts and to connect Users directly with Customer Support, without exposing either party's actual phone number.
Contacts Runtime Optional — used only if the User chooses to invite contacts through the referral feature or share a booking with a contact. The app does not access contacts unless this feature is actively used.
Notifications Runtime To send booking confirmations, Expert assignment and arrival alerts, payment updates, offers, and safety or grievance-related communications.
Microphone Runtime Used only if the User records or sends a voice note while raising a support query or providing feedback. Not used for continuous or background audio recording.
Internet and Network State Normal (no prompt) Required for the app to function — connecting to SuperHerooo servers for bookings, payments, and support, and to check network connectivity.
Device and App Information Normal (no prompt) Used for fraud prevention, security, app diagnostics, and improving app stability and performance.

11.3 Background Location

Background location access, where requested, is used strictly to enable live tracking of an assigned Expert's arrival and to verify login/logout at the service location as described in Clause 16.4. It is not used to track Users when the app is not actively facilitating a booking, and is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

11.4 Managing Permissions

Users can view, grant, or withdraw any Android permission at any time through their device settings. Withdrawing a permission may limit the following, depending on the permission withdrawn:

  • Location — service address auto-detection, live Expert tracking, and insurance login/logout verification may not function correctly
  • Camera / Storage — KYC uploads, profile photos, and support attachments may need to be completed manually or may be unavailable
  • Phone — masked in-app calling between Customers and Experts may be unavailable, though bookings can still be managed through in-app chat where offered
  • Notifications — Users may not receive timely booking, payment, or safety alerts

11.5 No Unnecessary Data Collection

SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED does not request Android permissions beyond what is reasonably necessary for the features described above, does not use device permissions to collect data unrelated to the Platform's services, and does not sell permission-derived data to third parties. This section should be read together with Section 10 (Privacy and Data Protection) and the SuperHerooo Privacy Policy, which governs how any data collected through these permissions is stored, used, and protected.

16. Expert Insurance, Health Verification & Accidental Death Benefit

16.1 Nature of the Insurance Scheme

SuperHerooo may facilitate the enrollment of eligible Experts under a Group Personal Accident Insurance Policy, or any other insurance policy, issued and underwritten by a licensed third-party insurance company ("Insurer").

The insurance coverage is provided solely by the Insurer and is governed by the terms, conditions, exclusions, claim procedures, and policy wording issued by the Insurer from time to time.

SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED is not an insurance company and does not underwrite, guarantee, or directly provide insurance coverage. Its role is limited to facilitating enrollment, maintaining eligibility records, and assisting in claim coordination, wherever applicable.

Any insurance benefit, including the Accidental Death Benefit, shall be payable only by the Insurer, subject to successful claim verification, policy terms, and the Insurer's approval. The amount payable, if any, shall be determined exclusively by the Insurer under the applicable policy and shall not be treated as a fixed or guaranteed amount by SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED.

16.2 Eligibility

An Expert shall be eligible for insurance coverage only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

  • The Expert has successfully completed SuperHerooo's KYC verification and mandatory background verification
  • The Expert has submitted valid identity and address documents
  • The Expert is an active and approved Expert registered on the SuperHerooo Platform
  • The Expert has accepted a booking through the SuperHerooo Platform
  • The Expert has successfully logged into the assigned booking through the SuperHerooo application
  • The applicable insurance policy is active on the date of the incident

16.3 Health Verification by Mediator

Before assigning an Expert to any work, the SuperHerooo Mediator shall verify that the Expert appears physically fit and capable of performing the assigned work.

If the Expert appears ill, injured, intoxicated, or medically unfit, the Mediator shall not assign the work and shall immediately inform the SuperHerooo Supervisor.

If the Mediator knowingly assigns a visibly unfit Expert and such negligence directly results in the Expert's death or serious injury, the Mediator shall be held responsible under SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED's internal policies. SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED reserves the right to recover any compensation, damages, losses, or expenses from the Mediator, where permitted by applicable law, and to initiate disciplinary and legal proceedings against the Mediator.

16.4 Coverage Period

Insurance coverage shall commence immediately after the Expert successfully logs into an assigned booking through the SuperHerooo Platform and shall continue only until the booking is completed or the Expert logs out, whichever is earlier.

Travel to or from the Customer's service location — whether before login, after logout, or at any other time — shall not be covered under this insurance benefit. For clarity, no insurance benefit, compensation, or payment of any kind shall be payable in the event of an Expert's death or injury occurring while travelling to or from a service location, regardless of whether the booking had been accepted or was scheduled.

16.5 Accidental Death Benefit

In the unfortunate event of the accidental death of an eligible Expert occurring strictly during the insurance coverage period defined in Clause 16.4 (i.e., after login and before logout/completion of the booking), the third-party Insurer, subject to its policy terms and claim approval process, shall pay the applicable Accidental Death Insurance Benefit to the registered nominee or, where no nominee has been registered, to the lawful legal heir(s).

SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED shall only facilitate the claim submission process and assist in coordinating the required documentation with the Insurer. SUPERHEROOO PRIVATE LIMITED shall not be responsible for approving, rejecting, delaying, or paying any insurance claim, as such decisions rest solely with the Insurer.

16.6 Mandatory Reporting of Death

In the event of the death of an Expert during the insurance coverage period, the incident must be reported to the designated SuperHerooo Supervisor within one (1) hour from the time the incident becomes known.

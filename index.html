Everything you need to know about Superherooo.

Superherooo is India's premium on-demand everyday-help platform. We connect you with background-verified local partners for errands, pickup-drop help, queue waiting, and daily assistance.

Every hero on our platform undergoes a rigorous 3-step verification process: government ID verification, police background check, and a skill assessment test conducted by our expert team. Only the top performers make it onto the platform.

Simply download the Superherooo app, select your service, choose a time slot, and confirm your booking. A verified Hero will arrive at your door at the scheduled time. You can also track your Hero in real-time through the app.

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with the service, contact our 24/7 support team within 24 hours and we'll arrange a free re-service or provide a full refund — no questions asked.

Click on "Become a Hero" in the navigation menu or visit our Partner page. Fill in your details, select your primary skill, and submit your application. Our team will contact you within 48 hours to guide you through the onboarding and verification process.

Superherooo is currently operational in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune. We are rapidly expanding and will be live in 20+ cities by the end of 2026. Download the app and check availability in your area.
